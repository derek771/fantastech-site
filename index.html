What we do

Six things, done
all the way through.

We size it, we sell it, we stock it, and we put it in. Nobody gets handed off to somebody else halfway through the job.

System design & sizing

We start from your actual loads — well pump, shop tools, barn fans, the house — and size the array, inverter and battery bank to carry them. You get the math, not a guess.

Installation

Ground mount or roof, grid-tied or fully off-grid. Derek is on the job himself, start to finish, and the crew doesn't leave until the system runs.

Off-grid & battery backup

Hybrid inverters and LiFePO4 banks sized to carry you through an outage — or to run a place that has never had a utility line to it.

Agricultural solar

Irrigation and livestock pumps, barn fans, feed equipment, poultry houses. One customer's system carried a 20,000‑hen barn plus the house for two and a half months without the generator starting.

Solar refrigeration

DC refrigerators and chest freezers built to run off a panel and a battery. Real cold storage for homes, cabins and farms with no grid power and no propane bill.

Wholesale & dealer supply

Panels, inverters, batteries, racking and balance-of-system at dealer pricing for installers and resellers, out of our own Goshen warehouse.

How a job goes

Five steps, in order.

No mystery and no surprise invoice at the end. Here is exactly how it runs from the first phone call to the day it's making power.

1

You call

Tell us what you want to run and roughly what you're spending. Ten minutes on the phone usually settles the size.

2

Load list

We write down every load — pumps, fans, tools, the house — and figure the daily kilowatt-hours off it.

3

Itemized quote

You get a real document with every panel, breaker and foot of wire on it. No lump sum, nothing hidden.

4

Build & install

We pull the order from our own shelf, build what needs building in the shop, and set it on site.

5

Support

Monitoring set up before we leave, and a phone number that reaches the man who built it.
