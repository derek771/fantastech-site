Questions
Can a freezer really run on solar?

Yes. The DC models run straight off a 12 or 24 V battery bank with no inverter. The 120 V models run off your inverter. We size the panels and batteries to keep it cold through the night.

How do freezers get to me?

They ship by truck. Call 574-218-1973 for a shipping quote, or pick one up in Goshen.

Do you install it, or just sell it?

Both. We design and install in Elkhart, LaGrange, Noble and Kosciusko counties, and we ship equipment nationwide.

Can I pick it up in Goshen?
