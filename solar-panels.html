Questions
Do you sell full pallets and containers?

Yes. Panels go out by the piece, the pallet or the container. Ask us about container pricing, and installers can ask about becoming a wholesale partner.

Can you put the panels up too?

Yes. We set ground mounts and roof arrays, and we size the strings so the panels match your inverter.

Do you install it, or just sell it?

Both. We design and install in Elkhart, LaGrange, Noble and Kosciusko counties, and we ship equipment nationwide.

Can I pick it up in Goshen?

Yes. Our shop and warehouse is at 3645 S 1200 W, Goshen, IN 46528, open Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Saturday by appointment.

Do you have dealer pricing?

Yes. Installers and resellers can apply for tiered dealer pricing: entry wholesale, wholesale and distributor. Call 574-218-1973 or use the form on our home page.
